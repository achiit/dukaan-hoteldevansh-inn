About the hotel

Hotel Devansh Inn is a small, family-run budget hotel steps from Varanasi Cantt Railway Station. It suits travellers arriving on a late train, leaving on an early one, or staying a few nights. We keep the rooms clean, the prices fair, and the front desk open around the clock. We have been welcoming guests for around 3–5 years, including tourists, families and business travellers.

Hosted by Amarnath Gupta.
